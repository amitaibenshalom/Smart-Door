Privacy Policy for TzufGuard

Last updated: March 31, 2026

Amitai Ben Shalom ("we," "our," or "us") operates the TzufGuard mobile application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

TzufGuard is designed as a personal utility tool to monitor the status of a specific door. The app communicates directly with a privately owned, self-hosted server to retrieve door sensor data. No account creation is required, and no user analytics, location data, or personal identifiers are tracked or transmitted to any third parties.

2. Log Data and Analytics

The Service does not use third-party analytics tools, crash reporters, or advertising frameworks that would collect log data from your device.

3. Security

We value your trust in using our Service. The communication between the app and the server utilizes standard HTTPS protocols to ensure the status of the door is transmitted securely. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

4. Links to Other Sites

This Service may contain a link to the project's open-source GitHub repository. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. Note that these external sites are not operated by us. Therefore, we strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of those websites.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted.
